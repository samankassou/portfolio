The ENSPD logo is used in its official site's footer; the IUT Douala and IUT Ngaoundéré logos appear in their website headers. Each mark identifies its corresponding existing education entry, with no claim of endorsement. The institutions retain rights to their logos; these assets are not covered by the Devicon MIT license.

Original files, colors, and proportions are preserved. Logos are served locally and displayed with object-contain on white in both light and dark themes. The IUT Douala and Ngaoundéré source images are small, so their display is limited to 80 px.
